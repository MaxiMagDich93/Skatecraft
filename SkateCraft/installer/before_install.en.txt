SkateCraft - walk skate.'s world as your Minecraft player, and build in it.

Before you install, you need:

  1. skate. on Steam, on your Steam account. SkateCraft runs it through ReSkate
     (the fan-made launcher and mod platform for skate.).
  2. Minecraft: Java Edition, on your Microsoft account.
  3. About 16 GB of free space: about 14 GB for skate. (unless you take the files
     from a ReSkate you already have) and about 1-2 GB for Minecraft.
  4. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

What gets installed:

  - A ReSkate of its own, with SkateCraft in it, in the folder you choose next.
    Your normal ReSkate (if you have one) stays as it is.
  - SkyCraft's Minecraft (Prism Launcher with a "SkateCraft" instance) in
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\SkateCraft. Minecraft itself and Java are downloaded by
    Prism the first time you sign in.

Nothing from EA, Mojang or Microsoft is included.
